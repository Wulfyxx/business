What are we?

The Dev Network is a small community* of developers and builders, both leasing out development work and builders as well as selling premade assets such as maps to anyone who might need them! Additionally, we also have resources for those interested in starting a server for a game- We can solve a variety of needs, whether it's figuring out where to start, where to go, or how to get there. We also offer premade code for common needs such as chat moderation and player management.
